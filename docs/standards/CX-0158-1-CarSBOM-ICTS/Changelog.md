All notable changes to CX-0158-1 will be documented in this file.

## [1.1.0] - R26.09 (CX-Neptune)

### Added

- Indication of non-normative Content for the whole section 1.
- Information that the standard acts as use-case extension for CX-0158 in section 1.3.
- Reference to the base standard CX-0158 in section 1.4.

### Changed

- Degree of obligation from "but they do not contradict or replace any baseline definitions and requirements" to "but they MUST NOT contradict or replace any baseline definitions and requirements" in section 2.

### Removed

- Indication of non-normative content for the subsections 1.1, 1.2, 1.4.
- Indication of normative content for subsection 1.3, 2.4.
- Redundant conformity assessment criteria in section 1.3 (see conformity assessment criteria in standard CX-0158).
- Redundant terminological definitions in section 1.4 (see terminological definitions in standard CX-0158).

## [1.0.0] - R26.06 (CX-Neptune (Preview))

### Added

- Initial version of Car SBOM ICTS Standard
